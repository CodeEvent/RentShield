1.Who we are

The Controller is [RentShield — legal entity name, trade licence no., registered address, Emirate]. For privacy matters, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@rentshield.ae.

2.Personal data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, password.
  • Identity-verification data: Emirates ID / passport details and, where used, a photo or liveness check, processed to confirm you are the owner or an authorised agent. This may include biometric data, which is “sensitive personal data” under the PDPL.
  • Tenancy & document data: owner and tenant details, the registered Ejari contract, property details, grounds, dates and amounts you enter to generate a notice.
  • Transaction data: payment details (processed by our payment provider) and billing records.
  • Technical & usage data: device, IP address, log data and cookies (see our Cookie Policy).

4.Identity verification

Identity verification may be carried out using a specialist provider. Where this involves biometric or other sensitive personal data, we process it only with your explicit consent and only for verification, and we apply additional safeguards as required by the PDPL.

5.Sharing & processors

We share personal data only as needed to run the Service, with processors bound to protect it, including: identity-verification, electronic-signature, payment, hosting and analytics providers, and — where you instruct it — a Notary Public or registered-mail provider. We may disclose data where required by UAE law or a competent authority (for example, the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre or a court). We do not sell personal data.

6.Cross-border transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UAE, we do so only in line with the PDPL — to jurisdictions with an adequate level of protection, or under appropriate safeguards (such as contractual clauses) or a permitted exception. Details of our processors and transfer mechanisms are available on request.

7.Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above and to meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations under UAE law, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Specific retention periods are set out in our internal retention schedule.

8.Your rights

Subject to the PDPL, you have the right to: be informed; access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; receive your data in a portable form; and not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. To exercise any right, contact dpo@rentshield.ae; we will respond within the period required by law.

9.Security & breaches

We apply technical and organisational measures (including encryption in transit, access controls and audit trails) to protect personal data. If a personal-data breach occurs that meets the PDPL threshold, we will notify the UAE Data Office and affected individuals as and when the law requires.

10.Children

The Service is for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

11.Contact & complaints

Contact our DPO at dpo@rentshield.ae. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office, the federal authority responsible for data protection.
