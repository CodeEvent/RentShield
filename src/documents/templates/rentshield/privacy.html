This notice explains what RentShield collects when you use the service, why, and how it is handled. RentShield helps Dubai property owners verify their identity and generate tenancy notices.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the passkey/authenticator you set up to sign in.
  • Identity data: your passport (or ID) chip data and a live selfie, used once to verify you are who you say you are. Biometric data is template-protected and does not leave the server.
  • Property and tenancy details: the information you enter to generate a notice (owner, tenant, unit, Ejari number, dates, reason).
  • Documents: the notices and files you generate or upload, stored in your own document vault.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card details.

Why we use it

  • To confirm your identity and keep your account secure.
  • To generate correct, bilingual tenancy notices and store them for you.
  • To provide optional add-ons you request (e-signature, legal review, notary).
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Who processes it

We use trusted services to run RentShield, including an identity-verification provider, an authentication provider, and a payment provider. They process data only to provide their part of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and documents for as long as your account is active, and as required by law. You can ask us to delete data that we are not legally required to retain.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or request deletion of your data.
  • Manage how you sign in from your account security settings.
  • RentShield is self-hostable, so your data can stay under your control.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising trackers.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at the email address on your account.
