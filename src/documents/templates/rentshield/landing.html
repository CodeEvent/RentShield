FAQ

Questions owners ask

Is this legal advice?

No. RentShield prepares a correctly-formatted, statute-cited notice and routes you to lawful service. For a contested matter, instruct a licensed advocate.

Does the e-signature serve the tenant?

No — the signature certifies the owner. Legal service under Article 25(3) requires a Notary Public, registered mail, or a court bailiff. RentShield routes you to those.

Why is the period 12 months?

For eviction at the end of a tenancy on grounds such as owner's use, sale, demolition or major renovation, Law 33/2008 requires 12 months' notice via notary or registered mail. Non-payment during the term is different — 30 days.

Do you support agencies and portfolios?

Yes. The dashboard tracks every notice, deadline and service method across a managed portfolio, with volume pricing.
