Why we built this

A bad notice helps no one

Most notices that get disputed fail for a small reason: the wrong number of days, the wrong way of sending it, or no proof of who sent it. When that happens at the Rental Dispute Settlement Centre, the months you waited count for nothing.

We think the notice should be the one thing you never have to worry about. So we get the law, the dates, both languages, and the sender's identity right for you.

FAQ

Common questions

Why do I need to prove my identity?
A notice carries legal weight, so we check the person sending it is real. You do it once, with your passport chip and a selfie, and a Notary Public approves it. Every notice you make after that is tied to your verified identity.
How much does it cost?
AED {{ base_price_aed }} per notice, with no subscription. You can add extras like a certified e-signature or a legal review only if you need them.
How long is the notice period in Dubai?
It depends on the reason. Sale, personal use, demolition, or major renovation need 12 months. Non-payment or illegal subletting need 30 days. We pick the right one for you.
Does the notice need to be notarised?
To serve it, the law accepts three ways: a Notary Public, registered mail with proof of receipt, or a court bailiff. An email or text message does not count. Our add-ons can handle the official part for you.
Is RentShield RERA compliant?
Yes. Every notice cites UAE Law No. 33 of 2008 and the exact article it uses, the same law RERA and the Rental Dispute Settlement Centre apply.
Can a tenant dispute a notice?
Yes. A tenant can challenge it at the RDSC. A notice that is correct and from a verified sender is far harder to dispute. None of this is legal advice.
