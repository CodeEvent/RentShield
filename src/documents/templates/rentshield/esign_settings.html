What counts as lawful service

Certified e-signature

A cryptographically signed PDF (PKCS#7). Proves who signed and that the document is unaltered — but is not, on its own, lawful service of an eviction notice under Dubai Law No. 33 of 2008.

Real notarisation / service

Service via Notary Public, registered mail, or court bailiff is what the statute requires. RentShield keeps these distinct and never presents an e-signature as legal service.
