A notice only holds up if the person sending it is who they say they are. RentShield proves that once, then builds every tenancy notice on it: the right law, both languages, a verified sender.

Before you start

If you're new, you'll need:

  • an email address you can check now
  • a valid photo ID (passport, national ID, driving licence, or residence visa)
  • your phone with the RentShield app, for the identity check at the end
There's no password to create. A passkey (your face, fingerprint, or device screen lock) is what signs you in from now on, and your biometric data never leaves the server.
