1.What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and SDKs. They let a site function, remember your choices, and understand how it is used.

3.Consent & legal basis

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and its implementing regulations, we rely on your consent for non-essential cookies. When you first visit, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies through our consent banner, and you can change your choice at any time. Strictly necessary cookies are used on the basis of our legitimate operation of the Service.

4.Third-party technologies

Some cookies are set by third parties that help us run the Service, for example Google reCAPTCHA (bot protection) and analytics providers. Their use of data is governed by their own policies; for reCAPTCHA, the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

5.Managing cookies

  • Use our consent banner / “Cookie settings” link to accept or reject categories and to withdraw consent.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Service working.
