Editorial Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · United Arab Emirates & the Emirate of Dubai

Draft template, not legal advice. Describe only the review process you actually operate. Review with a UAE-licensed lawyer before publishing.

This Editorial Policy explains how RentShield produces, sources, reviews and keeps current the legal information and notice templates on the Service, so you understand what our content is, and is not.

1.Scope & purpose

It covers the explanatory content, notice templates, statutory periods and Article references the Service provides for Dubai tenancy notices. The goal is clear, accurate, plain-language information that reflects current UAE and Dubai law.

2.Sources

  • Primary UAE and Dubai legislation, in particular Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended by Law No. 33 of 2008, and related decrees and regulations.
  • Official publications of the UAE government, the Dubai Land Department and the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre (RDSC).
  • Official gazettes and government portals for amendments.

We prefer primary and official sources and cite the relevant Article where a notice relies on it.

3.How content is written & reviewed

  • Content is drafted in plain English and Arabic and mapped to the specific statutory provision it relies on.
  • Legal content and templates are reviewed by, or under the supervision of, a UAE-qualified legal reviewer before release. (State your actual reviewer / firm and credentials here.)
  • Changes to the law trigger a review of the affected templates and periods.

4.Keeping content current

We monitor for amendments and periodically review templates and statutory periods. Each legal page shows a “last updated” date. Despite this, law and official practice can change between reviews; see our Disclaimer.

5.Information, not advice

Our content is general legal information and software output, not legal advice, and not a substitute for a licensed UAE advocate. RentShield is not a law firm.

6.Independence & corrections

Editorial content is kept separate from commercial and advertising considerations. If you believe something is inaccurate or out of date, tell us at editorial@rentshield.ae; we review reports and correct confirmed errors, updating the “last updated” date.
